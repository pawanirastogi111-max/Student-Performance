🧠 Diagnostic Feature Impact Analysis
  • Productivity Score (99% Correlation): The most influential feature for forecasting final exam scores. Reflects focus efficiency and sleep recovery minus burnout.
  • Focus Index (98% Correlation): Concentration levels. Driven upward by active self-study and sleep hygiene; degraded by digital screen overload.
  • Study Hours (80% Correlation): Direct daily study time acts as the primary foundation for student academic capability.
  • Circadian Sleep Deficit: Sleep deviation directly suppresses the Focus Index and feeds burnout levels, creating a double-penalty effect on final grades.
